# Lock It Down: Cybersecurity Awareness Guide

A short guide to help people stay safe online.

## About
Lock It Down is a cybersecurity awareness guide for students and staff.
It explains three common online threats (phishing, password attacks and
identity theft), gives real-life examples of each, and lists simple steps
to stay safe. It also includes a quick checklist and a template for
reporting incidents.

## Contents
- docs/phishing.md: fake messages and how to spot them
- docs/password-attacks.md: how passwords get stolen and how to protect them
- docs/identity-theft.md: how personal details get misused
- checklists/security-checklist.md: quick safety checklist

## Key threats covered
Phishing, password attacks, identity theft
