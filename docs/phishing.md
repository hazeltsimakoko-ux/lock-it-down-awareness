# PHISHING

## What it is
Phishing is when an attacker sends a fake email, SMS or message that
pretends to come from a trusted source, such as a bank, a school or an
employer. The goal is to trick you into clicking a link or handing over
personal information like passwords or card details.

## Example
You get an SMS saying "Your bank account has been suspended. Verify now
at secure-bank-login.example." The link opens a fake page that looks
real. When you type your username and password, the attacker steals them.

## Warning signs
- Urgent or threatening language ("Act now or lose your account")
- Generic greetings such as "Dear customer"
- Spelling mistakes or odd-looking logos
- A sender address or link that doesn't match the real organisation

## How to protect yourself
- Don't click links in unexpected messages. Go to the official website yourself.
- Check the sender's real email address and hover over links before clicking.
- Never share passwords, PINs or OTPs. Real organisations don't ask for them.
- Turn on multi-factor authentication (MFA) on your accounts.

## What to do if you clicked
1. Change your password straight away.
2. Watch your accounts for unusual activity.
